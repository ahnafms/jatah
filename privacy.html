Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

What jatah reads

With the owner's permission, jatah uses read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) to read payment notification emails from senders the owner chooses, such as BCA and Mandiri. It does not read other emails, and it never sends, changes or deletes email.

Where data goes

Everything jatah reads stays on the owner's iPhone. There is no jatah server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. Google sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

If the owner turns on smart sorting, only shop names are sent to OpenRouter, using the owner's own key, to suggest a category. Amounts, dates and bank details are not sent.

Removing access

Disconnect an account in jatah's Settings, or remove jatah's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
